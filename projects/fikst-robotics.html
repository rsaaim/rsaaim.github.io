[ UNDER NDA ]Client name, product images and part geometry are withheld. This page describes the engineering problems and my approach in general terms.

Liquid-ingress seal

Liquid reaching the robot's electronics caused shorts that required field replacements. I designed an injection-molded silicone seal to keep liquid out of the electronics.

Drop-in cable management

Technicians were routing and replacing cables one at a time. I designed an injection-molded nylon cable-management part with alignment features, so the full wiring harness drops in as one unit. It drastically cut the time spent routing and replacing cables.

Chassis changes

I modified the chassis to fit new hardware and make the robot easier to service, and validated the changes with bolted-joint and tolerance-stack analysis.

Getting it built

I create and release the engineering drawings, and work with suppliers on DFM and with the production line on design changes. Those changes reduced assembly issues and cycle time.
